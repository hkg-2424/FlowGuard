Welcome to FlowGuard

FlowGuard is a local-first Windows focus assistant engineered to help you achieve and sustain deep flow without shame, invasive blocking, or cloud telemetry.

100% Local & Private: All activity monitoring runs entirely on your PC using native Windows Win32 APIs.
Zero Configuration: No complex background servers or browser setup required. It just works.
🎯

One-Click Workflows

Focus is contextual. FlowGuard organizes your day into Workflows (like Study, Deep Work, Coding, or Reading) that define authorized applications and websites.

Pre-Configured Presets: Launch Study, Coding, or Deep Work with 1 click.
Customizable: Easily create your own workflows with specific apps and domain rules.
⏳

The 30-Second Grace Window

Human attention naturally drifts. FlowGuard embraces this reality through the 30-second Grace Window instead of harsh, sudden punishment.

Gentle Runway: When you open a distracting app or website, FlowGuard starts an animated 30-second grace timer.
No Panic: You have 30 full seconds to recognize the distraction and return to your workflow.
🔄

Pull-Back Resilience & Quality Score

Focus isn’t the absence of distraction—it’s the speed of recovery. FlowGuard actively rewards Pull-Backs!

Self-Correction Celebrations: Returning to your allowed workflow before the 30s timer expires logs a "Pull-Back" self-correction.
Protected Quality Score: Self-correcting preserves your 100% Focus Quality Score!
🤝

Commitment Contracts

Set the level of accountability that fits your mood and willpower:

Standard: Free, low-friction focus. End session anytime.
Accountable Breakable: Requires typing a thoughtful reflection reason before quitting early.
Unbreakable (Ulysses): Locked in until your target timer ends. For your most critical deep work blocks.
